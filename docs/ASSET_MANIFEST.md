# LEXORYN — cinematic asset manifest

The `data/assets.ts` registry is the frontend replacement boundary. `asset-manifest.json` contains exact source filenames, SHA-256 hashes, native dimensions and optimized-copy sizes. All 18 approved originals are preserved byte-for-byte under `references/`; source files outside the project remain untouched. Existing plates below are **provided**, not generated by this implementation. References inform style; they do not override the current story bible or authorize unrelated actions.

## Phase 1 scene mapping

| Chapter | Final scene source / technique | Reference anchor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 First contact | Real R3F planet with original procedural materials, cloud layer and atmosphere | Engineered mineral world for wear/rim light; atlas and energy rupture for mineral/emissive restraint |
| 02 Approach | Actual 3D camera motion past lit station/satellites and broken moon | New Concord orbital station for ring/hub/spoke/solar-array hierarchy |
| 03 Entry | Layered 3D/composited cloud depth, changing light and color | New storm cockpit for practical amber/cyan atmosphere; generated cloud plate if present |
| 04 First reveal | Actual approved wide city plate with depth-layer foreground flight | New Velair Prime aerial; earlier cloud city for atmospheric continuity |
| 05 Arrival | Wide city establishing shot framed from a real-time observation platform | Same capital plate; hangar/sanctum establish transport and civic continuity for later phases |

Planet references are never substituted for the interactive exterior. A scenic city background is an image plate, not falsely described as a fully modeled city. Full vehicle/civilization exploration, characters, hidden core and crisis remain Phase 2–4.

## Delivery policy

Provided images are retained at native pixel dimensions as quality-94 WebP, with quality-90 maximum-640px responsive copies. Do not upscale the 292×232 storm thumbnail. Many screenshots are roughly 1100px wide, so large-screen detail is source-limited. Keep the original aspect ratio; intentional viewport crops may be used for cinematic framing but never stretch. For replacement plates request 3840×2160 or larger where appropriate, and preserve exact framing/material continuity. Generated textures need seam checks, mipmaps and appropriate color-space settings; raw maps should not be substituted for physically meaningful channels.
