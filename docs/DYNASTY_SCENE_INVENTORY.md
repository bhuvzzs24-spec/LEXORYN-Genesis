# Alien Dynasty scene and source audit

Baseline `25478ed98c6404bfefa4a4f709f57991159be710`. Audited12 November2026. This records the **existing sources before redesign integration**, not a claim that all images have already been replaced. All56 scenes remain listed. Main image files were decoded and measured; five contact sheets were visually inspected, with individual full-size checks for the old Myr ceremony, comic, food atlas and fallbacks.

## Critical findings

- Existing environments are native1672×941. They cannot truthfully be called native4K. Most public crowds, council delegates and character portraits are human. Changing registry species labels will not change their pixels.
- Full-body Kaor, Sorya, Elara and Vaelora must be replaced for Vaelori anatomy; existing human Aaryan/Nyra can remain. Supporting atlas needs species-aware replacements.
- The comic strip visibly depicts human Elara, Nyra and Aaryan. It needs revised alien political participation and consistent new identities.
- Myr ceremony contains old quadrupedal reptilian delegates and a human-majority audience. The ecosystem also used “Myr” for herd wildlife. Those conflict with the revised native intelligent species.
- The still-image planet fallback remains a stale1280×720 pre-mineral globe. It does not match the current real3D mineral shader.
- Food/attire atlas cells are roughly512×512, gear cells512×341 and boot cells724×724. They are interactive alpha cutouts, not2048px individual product portraits.
- Scene49 needs a new balcony image and removal of reused circular machinery. The physical Codex machinery remains valid in its own sequence.
