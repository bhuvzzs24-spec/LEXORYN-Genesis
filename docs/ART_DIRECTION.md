## World appearance

A dark mineral world transformed by intelligence over centuries. Every shot should communicate scale through atmospheric depth, recurring infrastructure and physically legible materials. Cinematic realism is the target; the real-time implementation remains a foundation to be judged from actual rendered captures, not a claim of final film production quality. No cartoon portraits, arbitrary neon purple, floating icon planets, stock city photographs or unmodified Earth geography.

Three connected climate zones explain three related visual identities. Pyravar is a rain-shadow and high-insolation desert belt: black rock, heat haze, reflective metallic trim and buried reservoirs. Cryonth occupies the poles: cyan glacier masses, silver insulation, dark basalt geothermal refuges and compact research settlements. Aerion Meridia occupies inhabited temperate corridors: storm-gray air, bronze civic forms, graphite towers, suspended farms and controlled cyan light. The relationships between latitude, sunlight, polar freezing, wind corridors and geothermal refuges are scientifically informed speculative design; they are not a climate simulation or measurements of a real exoplanet. Configurable assumptions live in `data/world.ts` for organizer mystery-card adaptation.

## Planet exterior

The exterior must remain a true three-dimensional object: asymmetric original continents, near-black cobalt seas, obsidian relief, restrained golden engineering faults, differentiated polar ice, uneven blue-violet storm systems, night-only settlement lights, thin illuminated atmosphere, real orbital geometry and a broken moon. The new engineered-sphere screenshot informs weathering, silhouette and ancient construction but is never used as a flat substitute planet. The rocky ground-level view in that plate is surreal composition rather than literal orbital physics.

Use separate surface, cloud and atmosphere layers. Maintain a coherent star direction, day-night terminator and relative surface/cloud motion. Engineering channels should appear embedded, sparse and aged rather than uniformly painted neon. Ice, ocean, rock and metal need distinct roughness and reflectance. Orbital structures should have a visible structural hierarchy—hub, spokes, ring, docking modules, radiators and solar arrays—not identical gold boxes. Broken-moon fragments should share material and a localized orbital arc; visual orbits are cinematic approximations, not an n-body simulation.

## Lighting and material rules

One hard warm stellar source establishes silhouettes. Cool space fill stays weak. Amber practical lamps, cyan interfaces and white-blue engines have specific functions, not blanket emission. Preserve shadows, black levels and quiet surfaces. Avoid intense bloom that erases machined detail. Thin atmospheric scattering should brighten the illuminated limb and fade naturally on the dark side. City shots use late amber sunlight, cyan haze and deep material texture. Rain, fog, droplets and clouds must respect the light direction.

Material hierarchy: rough obsidian and basalt; brushed charcoal titanium; patinated bronze; anodized silver; heat-resistant ceramic; layered woven technical fabrics; clear instrument glass. Edge wear belongs at contact surfaces. Do not cover every object with decorative glowing lines. The original spacecraft reference sets the standard for panel scale and docking structure.

## Architecture

Velair Prime combines monumental tapered stabilizer towers and crowned civic silhouettes with elevated transit, layered plazas and dense inhabited districts. Black titanium and bronze dominate. The wide aerial city reference unifies carved domes with advanced facade systems, while the earlier cloud-city image establishes height and atmosphere. Cyan strips mark infrastructure and circulation rather than arbitrary decoration. Hanging bridges and rail corridors need supports or a consistent speculative suspension technology. The floating sanctum is a ceremonial district, not every building in the capital.

Pyravar: thick shaded walls, low wind-resistant masses, solar harvest planes and subterranean cooling. Cryonth: compact insulated forms, thermal joints, geothermal pillars and sheltered transport. Do not use identical towers with different color filters for all regions.

## Technology

Ancient planetary infrastructure is bronze/obsidian, embedded and infrastructural. Concord equipment is maintainable titanium/silver, with modular panels and restrained cyan status light. Amber indicates heat, energy or practical cabin illumination. Screens in final scene artwork should have no garbled text or Earth maps. Build readable UI with HTML rather than baking generated labels into artwork. G.P.T. will be a purposeful interpretive interface in Phase 3; do not expose a fake incomplete chat or dashboard in Phase 1.

## Character and costume continuity (Phase 3)

Preserve each approved character's face, age, silhouette, hair and role across shots. The defense portrait establishes articulated charcoal armor with flexible black underlayers and small cyan seams. Scientists, pilots, engineers and ceremonial leaders must not share the same warrior costume. The braided portrait's gold ear devices and cyan jewelry and the older specialist's transparent smart glasses are material references only until a consistent original cast is approved. The violet fantasy portrait in the collage is excluded from realistic final assets.

Climate drives clothing. Pyravar uses layered heat-fiber drapes, visors and reflective edges; Cryonth uses insulated coats, sealed gloves and thermal boots; Meridia uses fitted mobility fabrics, bronze-trimmed civic coats and wind-rated footwear. Rank is expressed through construction, trim and practical accessories. Boots, closures, gloves, tools and pockets should remain consistent from full-body views to close-ups. Never introduce a new face because a matching generation is difficult.

## Vehicle principles

A defined propulsion language joins the silver hangar shuttles, detailed orbital station and charcoal sky bike. Use recessed engines, glass canopies, modular service panels, plausible access points, restrained cyan propulsion and warm running lights. Craft follow defined lanes, bank purposefully and change apparent size with depth. Scout movement in Phase 1 is a small foreground proof of arrival; full traffic, transport interaction and city routes belong to Phase 2. The storm cockpit informs canopy thickness, weather-sealed gloves, rust utility sleeves and practical illuminated controls.

## Food and culture continuity (Phase 2)

Food follows agriculture: mist-grown sky kelp, nutrient-rich grain, geothermal cold crops, protected solar fruits and controlled fungal protein. Preserve consistent containers, market tools, storage technology and serving rituals. Avoid ordinary Earth meals merely renamed alien. The brass market plate provides textile/produce/material direction but its native traders are not automatically the canonical human cast. Convergence ceremony uses robe silhouettes, gold civic machinery, synchronized lights and restrained energy inscriptions. Culture must remain lived-in and materially connected to climate and technology.

## Negative prompts / rejection criteria

No cartoon, anime, chibi, flat illustration, toy plastic, simplified low-poly scene, glossy game avatar, random stock photo, generic Earth globe, familiar Earth continents, oversaturated purple, rainbow neon, excessive bloom, empty skyline, repeated cloned towers, arbitrary spires, impossible scale, distorted hands, duplicate limbs, inconsistent face, incoherent costume, unreadable generated labels, watermarks, logos, UI overlays, collage panels, excessive lens flare, warped perspective or blurry enlarged thumbnails. No CSS circles or gradient plates presented as cinematic artwork.

## Asset discipline

All 18 provided images were visually inspected; four new analyses are appended to the source catalog. Raw originals are copied byte-for-byte into `references/`. Source Downloads and TemporaryItems files stay unchanged. `asset-manifest.json` records SHA-256 and original dimensions. Web derivatives use quality-94 WebP at native dimensions plus 640px maximum-edge copies. No upscaling, stretching or destructive replacement. Semantic folder aliases are byte-identical WebP copies for future frontend stability.

The older atlas, Earth monitoring UI, tiny 292×232 storm spire and mixed-style character collage remain direction-only. They contain incompatible text, geography, low detail or stylization. Full-image inspectors preserve reference aspect ratios. A cinematic background may use an intentional viewport crop, but never stretch; keep the source aspect and camera framing recorded. New textures/plates must be swappable through `data/assets.ts`, with honest provided/generated/procedural/missing status and a graceful missing-asset path.
