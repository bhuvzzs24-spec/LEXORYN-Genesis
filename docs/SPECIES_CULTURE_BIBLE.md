# LEXORYN — Alien Dynasty design bible

Year3186. Canon is speculative fiction. `data/species.ts` and `data/characters.ts` are the configurable source of truth. The population targets are60% Vaelori,23% Myr/other natives and17% humans, not measured demographics. Runtime artwork must demonstrate that hierarchy; changing labels alone does not meet visual acceptance.

## History and political order

The native Myr evolved on Lexoryn and built ecological institutions before Vaelori ascendancy. Vaelori later established technological and political control through engineering, native knowledge and planetary intelligence. Humans arrived much later, adapting across generations as a small minority. ORIGIN is ancient, distributed and non-biological; it is not a human invention or human avatar.

The Lexorian Concord is Vaelori-led. Central government, defense, major infrastructure and ceremony are predominantly Vaelori institutions. Myr assemblies retain ecological authority, indigenous territorial institutions and custody of protected environmental systems. Humans have limited representation and substantial expertise in transport, engineering, research and survival work. They are neither uniformly helpless nor uniformly heroic. Vaelori include principled scientists and coercive administrators; Myr institutions also disagree over intervention, sovereignty and cooperation.

## Vaelori anatomy and visual invariants

A backward-elongated bifurcated cranial vault counterbalances an articulated upper spine. Continuous mineral brow shelves protect wide-set double-aperture eyes; low nasal breathing slits replace a protruding human nose. Recessed lateral acoustic membranes and fine mandibular sensory vanes replace human ears. Hands have six long digits arranged in two opposed triads, with visibly articulated joints. Feet use three contact points and a split heel. Long expressive forearms and a deliberate balanced gait must remain physically credible.

Graphite, pearl, obsidian and muted bronze pigmentation varies between individuals. Embedded mineral scutes and sparse cyan capillary bioluminescence are biological structures, not glowing makeup. Cranial width, brow shape, scute pattern, age wear and eye pigmentation distinguish individuals without changing species. Avoid blue humans, elves, recognizable film aliens, human ears or interchangeable faces.

Clothes fasten around cranial geometry and sensory clearances. Architectural shoulder forms and bronze ribs support biotechnology textiles. Jewelry uses cranial bridges, split-joint cuffs and six-digit signets rather than human necklaces indiscriminately stretched over aliens. Shoes support all three contacts and the split heel; gloves preserve six digits. Vehicles use long forearm reach, triad controls and adjustable seating.

## Myr anatomy and ecological practices

Myr have broad lens-shaped cranial mantles, three recessed sensory wells, throat exchange folds and paired branching chemosensory fans. They have no human nose/lip arrangement. Four-digit branching hands, broad compliant feet and low balanced posture differ visibly from Vaelori anatomy. Translucent mineral-organic tissues expose amber vascular webs; controlled green-cyan light patterns supplement chemical language. They are intelligent people, not insects with human heads or fantasy monsters.

Sensing combines chemical sampling, moisture interpretation and learned ecological inference. It is not omniscience. Exchange tissues need protected humid intervals. Grown membranes, living environmental ornaments and humidity reservoirs are protective technology. Foot coverings preserve compliant contact rather than imposing human boot geometry. Myr institutions govern the meaning and consent of biological signals; a glowing pattern is not automatically public telemetry.

## Human minority and constructed intelligences

Aaryan, Nyra, Kito and Tork remain physiologically human with original fictional identities. Practical weatherproof clothes, adapted transit harnesses, tools, magnetic boots and translation equipment express generations of adaptation. Human neighborhoods vary in wealth and political access. Workplaces provide human-compatible controls rather than pretending alien infrastructure is automatically ergonomic.

Bolt-7 remains an industrial robot with useful tool hands, service panels, replaceable magnetic feet and restrained mechanical expression. ORIGIN has no face or body. Responsive architecture, mineral networks, actual Codex machinery, environmental signals and spatial interfaces express its agency. Do not deform a still portrait to fake speech or walking.

- PYRAVAR: Vaelori solar governors oversee shielded low settlements, bronze heat ribs, shaded transit and underground water. Broad three-contact walkways, tall sensory-safe entrances, long-reach controls and storm-rated aviation express the ruling species. Myr ecological annexes use protected biological interfaces. Council composition should show Vaelori central leadership, Myr ecological delegations with meaningful territory/evidence authority, and smaller human worker/transport representation. Seating geometry must fit anatomy. Species-specific entrances and transit clearances need visible design logic, not arbitrary spikes.

## Food and agriculture

All six established foods remain. Exact compatibility and preparation are recorded in `speciesDiets`; these are fictional design constraints, not real nutrition guidance. Native food is not assumed safe for humans. Shared ceremonies offer distinct preparations and utensils rather than identical meals.

Aether grain is softened for Vaelori, filtered/fermented for Myr and cooked from a low-mineral human-safe cultivar. Cryo bloom provides Myr nutrient gel; human portions require cultivated detoxified tissue. Sky kelp is live and hydrated for Myr, rinsed for Vaelori and blanched after symbiont removal for humans. Solar fruit requires species-specific mineral/resin treatment. Mistwater broth has separate buffered, chemical-exchange and sanitized human recipes. Lumifungus cultivation separates Myr living mycelial exchange from cooked compatible caps and human-safe non-luminous strains.

Agriculture includes solar-shaded mineral crops, geothermal nutrient loops and mist-fed terraces. Clearly separate native ecological stewardship from industrial harvest. Tableware includes Vaelori triad-grip vessels, Myr exchange membranes and human utensils. Food preparation22 is daily work; feast31 is a communal cultural ceremony with the same named foods in different species-safe preparations.

## Fashion, schools, healthcare and daily society

Lysara designs Vaelori anatomical protective and ceremonial fashion. Myr coverings are grown ecological materials with sensing and humidity functions. Human clothes prioritize climate adaptation and practical interfaces. Jewelry, shoes and accessories must show craftsmanship, anatomical fastening and material wear; retain clear named-object inspection.

Schools teach multilingual interpretation, local biology, engineering and the consequences of political history. Healthcare has distinct anatomy-compatible treatment stations, environmental needs and consent protocols. Vaelori-led hospitals are not automatically human clinics with one alien standing nearby. Market stalls and housing should visually reflect the target population, with different body clearances, lighting tolerances and private communication needs.

## Festival, languages and philosophical customs

The Night of Convergence is primarily Vaelori ceremony, with Myr ecological rites and minority human participation. Vaelori ceremonial light is architectural and political; Myr patterned signals express ecological relationships and territorial memory; human work communities contribute transport and practical craft. Do not convert Myr ceremony into decorative spectacle without participants’ agency.

Vaelori speech combines sound and modest capillary emphasis. Myr use chemical and patterned-light communication with environmental context. Human translation devices interpret imperfectly. Elara’s scholarship concerns uncertainty and negotiation, not a universal magical translation button. Philosophical traditions debate stewardship, sovereignty, measured stability and who is excluded from optimization.
