# LEXORYN — world bible, Phase 2

**The living intelligence planet. Year 3186.** “The planet where intelligence became civilization.” This bible extends the existing Phase 1 canon without replacing its interactive 3D planet, climate profiles or discovery journey. Numerical assumptions remain configurable in `data/world.ts`; scene and interaction content belongs in `data/story.ts`. Organizer mystery-card changes override defaults. This is speculative fiction, not a verified climate simulation or a claim of official affiliation with ChatGPT or Codex.

## Shared civilization

The Lexorian Concord is a network of communities whose survival depends on ecological care and engineered climate corridors. Velair Prime is its capital in Aerion Meridia. The Codex Vein is ancient physical infrastructure: climate stabilizers, energy distribution and engineered ecosystem support beneath and across the crust. G.P.T., the Generative Planetary Thread, is the interpretive conversational intelligence that helps people compare conditions and coordinate decisions. It advises; accountable inhabitants and their councils decide. The discovery archive is dated 3186; settlement dates and contact chronology should remain adjustable rather than contradict organizer cards.

The main display follows a visitor moving through one world. Buildings, vehicles, food and garments must explain how people live there. Scene captions stay brief. Optional inspect controls hold the detail. The buried mystery, crisis, final Convergence ceremony, hero-character introductions and branching outcomes remain later-phase material. Phase 2 ends with festival preparation, not a crisis or completed Phase 3 ending.

## Planet and climate logic

Defaults carried forward: 0.92 Earth gravity, a 32-hour day, a 486-Earth-day year, 24° axial tilt and approximately19% exposed dark-teal basin coverage. Latitude-dependent insolation, prevailing winds, rain shadows, thermal inertia and geothermal heat are the qualitative design basis. The Codex Vein extends habitable corridors but does not remove heat, water or maintenance constraints. Enclosed seas are deep teal/near-black; the dominant land is asymmetric graphite and obsidian mineral terrain, with sparse gold engineering fractures and cyan ice at the poles. Regional weather is differentiated; the entire planet must not become uniformly purple or uniformly temperate.

| System | Pyravar | Cryonth | Aerion Meridia |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Climate | High solar load, dry wind, heat and charged dust | Polar cold, ice, geothermal refuges | Temperate wind corridors, rain, electrical storms |
| Land | Black-gold escarpments, desert basins, shaded canyons | Cyan glaciers, dark basalt fissures, ice caves | Cloud valleys, upland watersheds, sheltered coasts |
| Architecture | Thick shaded walls, low wind-resistant forms, buried cooling, reflective shields | Compact insulated domes, thermal locks, geothermal pillars, sheltered tunnels | Graphite stabilizer spires, bronze civic forms, hanging gardens, protected terraces |
| Water | Stored underground, carefully metered and reclaimed | Ice processed with waste heat; protected clean-water loops | Mist capture, reservoirs, rain and reuse networks |
| Energy | Solar harvesting and thermal storage | Geothermal heat and insulated power links | Grid coordination, atmospheric equipment, distributed storage |
| Agriculture | Shaded mineral-fruit terraces and subsurface hydroponics | Warm enclosed algae/hydroponic habitats | Mist-fed sky kelp, grains and sheltered terrace crops |
| Transport | Heat-shielded desert skimmers and service haulers | Insulated crawlers, sheltered freight and ice-rated access | Wind-rated hovercraft, orbital shuttles and elevated maglev transit |
| Clothing | Reflective heat-fiber layers, wrapped joints, visor, heat-walker soles | Thermal coats, sealed gloves, insulated boots, low-power markers | Fitted storm-resistant fabrics, mobility joints, sky-grip soles |
| Palette | Amber, black rock, patinated gold | Silver, cyan ice, deep polar black | Titanium, aged bronze, functional turquoise, storm gray |

These are interdependent regions, not isolated color-themed worlds. Pyravar exports solar power and heat-adapted produce; Cryonth contributes research, cold storage and geothermal engineering; Aerion Meridia coordinates transport, manufacturing and mist agriculture. Cargo routes must connect food, water, energy and technology systems. Do not imply that resource exchange is frictionless or that one region is self-sufficient.

## Velair Prime and the public realm

The capital silhouette joins tapered dark titanium stabilizers, aged-bronze civic crowns, ring transit platforms, dense terraces and canyon-like cloud depth. Functional cyan conduits mark navigation, climate and communication. Warm interior lights show inhabited scale. Residential gardens, public markets, healthcare and learning spaces should occupy the same architectural family. Civic ornament has a purpose: recognizable wayfinding, regional representation and ceremony.

The Three-Region Council uses three equal regional delegations with responsibility for heat/water, ice/energy and storm/transport resilience. Its imagery should convey deliberation and environmental stewardship through architecture and restrained spatial light. Avoid a conventional software dashboard. No council voting simulator is required in this phase. The Future Generation scene represents education, healthcare, science and community through a civic research environment; a generic modern classroom or hospital is not acceptable replacement art.

## A coherent transport language

Orbital and city craft share charcoal/silver structural panels, bronze service edges, recessed cyan propulsion, practical glass canopies and sparse amber running lamps. Aerial lanes have altitude separation and docking rules. Trains run on defined elevated infrastructure, not arbitrary floating roads. Near craft should occlude the world and change perspective; distant craft stay small. Desert skimmers need shields and dust tolerance. Cryonth crawlers need thermal cabins and traction. Cargo haulers trade elegance for capacity.

A still painting of a ship remains static. Independent flight must use actual mesh geometry or properly isolated alpha layers. The supplied MP4 illustrates compositing only. The supplied vehicle board guides silhouettes and materials; it is not a model pack. No transparent vehicle cutouts, rigged creatures or production glTF models are present in either supplied Phase 2 pack.

Scene hotspots inspect ingredients while the market or dining world remains visible. Supplied food crops are honest low-resolution detail references, never final 4K food photography.

## Clothing, footwear and everyday equipment

Costumes communicate climate, profession and rank. Keep construction, closures, gloves, boots, tools and wear patterns coherent across full-body and close views. Scientists, military personnel, pilots, engineers, medics and civic leaders must not share a generic armored warrior suit. The atelier uses real material samples, garment stands and purpose-specific full-body designs. Existing blurred garment crops establish direction, not finished cast portraits.

- **Neural bracelet:** personal authorization and safe neural/status interface; not omnipotent mind control.
- **Environmental visor:** wind, particulate and temperature readings; sealed to climate needs.
- **Translation pendant:** language mediation for work and travel; jewelry doubles as a readable social object.
- **Signal ring / communication brooch:** compact local signaling and identity; matching materials to the wearer’s role.
- **Climate earrings:** low-power personal environmental warning, especially ceremonial contexts where bulky equipment is unsuitable.
- **Scanner cuff:** field instrument for climate/infrastructure inspection; tools should have maintenance and calibration logic.
- **Medical sensor band:** consent-aware physiological monitoring used in care; no unsupported real medical claims.
- **Ceremonial crown:** Concord authority and cultural continuity, with restrained cyan crystal/instrument detail.
- **Heat-walker boots:** heat-resistant sole, reflective toe/edge, flexible ankle and dust seal.
- **Ice-grip boots:** thermal layers, traction, seals and low-power status markers.
- **Sky-grip footwear:** secured platform traction and impact support for aerial infrastructure.

The supplemental directory’s four files named `footwear/*` actually show torso garments. They must not be presented as boot designs. Clean boot detail crops are prepared instead from the newer costume/prop board. All detail images remain stills. Rotation is real only where a mesh model exists; otherwise label image inspection plainly.

## Native ecosystem and coexistence

Myr is used as a native ecological/symbiotic category, not proof of one fixed human-like species. The supplied “Myr luminous stag” is an animal design reference: a pale luminous quadruped with branched antlers. Aeralis is a membrane-winged glider; the avian reference has a feathered body. Root grass binds wet soils and interfaces with local nutrient cycles. Bioluminescent fungi recycle organic material. Different niches must retain distinct silhouettes, not become simple colored birds or copied deer everywhere.

Native life and controlled agriculture coexist through protected corridors, water stewardship and habitat limits. Technology supports ecosystems instead of covering every organism with circuit patterns. No transparent/rigger-ready wildlife source exists; procedural fireflies or drifting particles are environmental motion, not evidence of fully animated native animals. A final photorealistic ecosystem establishing plate and rigged/native creature assets remain production needs if not generated during this pass.

## Convergence preparation

The Night of Convergence marks regional network synchronization and shared responsibility. Phase 2 shows crews preparing civic lights, families gathering, transport adjusting and lantern-like drones beginning to rise. Thousands of lights may be efficiently instanced; the visual mass should remain legible and restrained. The full procession, feast, language of light and ceremony begin Phase 3. No auto-playing third-party soundtrack is supplied. Any sound must begin after interaction and retain mute; procedural ambient audio is distinct from licensed recorded music.

## Materials, motion and quality rules

Base palette: obsidian `#080B12`, navy `#0A1425`, graphite `#242A32`, bronze `#AC7A43`, gold `#F4C37A`, cyan `#59DBE7`, arctic blue `#6DAFE2`, muted violet `#6C5EAA`, ivory `#F1E8DB`. Hard warm sun, weak cool fill, localized emissive functions and real surface wear dominate. Avoid uniform neon, plastic armor, cloned towers, illustrative stock graphics, random actor substitution, baked garbled UI, watermarks and whole boards as scenery.

Scroll controls camera, layer depth, trajectories and light in reversible local scene progress. Idle motion can drift subtly until paused. Reduced motion must retain navigation and information with coherent still composition. The source scene map distinguishes mesh motion, layered stills, simple source preview loops and missing production assets. No canvas, shader or crop alone justifies a claim of final film quality.

## Provenance and raw input discipline

Both provided packs are copied byte-for-byte into matching `references/` subfolders. `data/phase2-assets.json` records source hashes, native dimensions, actual animation frame counts, quality classifications and exact crop rectangles. No source PNG/JPG/WebP is overwritten. Supplied docs and board text are reference content; current user instructions and editable LEXORYN canon govern implementation. User approval for this project is recorded; no public redistribution license is inferred or added. Raw packs and derivative art stay within the existing approved private workflow. Generated art is documented only after an actual image tool returns a usable file; planned names remain planned until verified.
