# Original LEXORYN cast continuity

Seven original assets were generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Six hero full-body cutouts provide all portrait crops and repeated scene appearances; the same pixels preserve facial identity across the experience. A five-figure transparent supporting atlas provides distinct cells for Kito Ren, Bolt-7, Dr. Thess Arin, Lysara Vehn and Tork Vel. ORIGIN has no human portrait and is represented by the real-time Codex Vein.

## Reference inspection and public distribution

Retained sources were found in the private `work/lexoryn-site-release/references` copies after the original Downloads folders were unavailable. The Genesis world-building atlas, Last Prompt asset atlas, character triptych and defense-commander reference were inspected for role, wardrobe and material continuity. These include recognizable actor-style likeness references and fantasy costume cues. None was copied into the public cast. New faces, clothing construction and insignia are original; no face-reference image was submitted to imagegen for these seven outputs. Restricted source images and derivatives stay outside the public repository.

## Registry contract

`data/characters.ts` records all12 identities, explicit face canon, wardrobe, material/color continuity, footwear, accessories/equipment, relationships, primary environment, crisis role and scene appearances. Asset dimensions are measured from actual files. Hero portraitCrop identifies a face/detail view of the same full-body asset, rather than a separately generated face; supporting portraitCrop identifies one full-body fifth of the atlas. Never apply a hero face crop to its full-body plane.

## Appearance and costume continuity rules

- Graphite and titanium structural forms, practical aged-bronze fasteners, restrained cyan diagnostic elements and realistic woven cloth tie the cast to the established environments. Armor is worn engineering equipment rather than a shiny fantasy suit.
- Aaryan retains the asymmetrical dark hair/silver streak, scar and bronze shoulder assembly. Kaor retains short silver hair, close beard and broad articulated armor. Sorya retains short dark curls, ivory coat and diagnostic cuffs. Nyra retains copper-braid undercut, utility suit and tools. Elara retains auburn braided coil, freckles, pendant and archival mantle. Vaelora retains the geometric bronze crown, low braided hair, dark structured robe and practical low-heel boots.
- Reuse the same cutout or pass the actual generated master as an identity reference before producing a new pose. Do not replace faces with unrelated future outputs.
- Support atlas order left-to-right is Kito, Bolt-7, Thess, Lysara, Tork. Each is isolated within a normalized0.2-width cell, with independent source alpha and clear full-body silhouette.
- Personalities and relationships are canon in the registry. Technology supports human/native decisions; ORIGIN is not a human hologram, celebrity avatar or magical face.

## Fidelity limits

These are detailed photorealistic generated cutout stills, not rigged 3D humans or talking video. Real perspective placement, independent parallax and lighting can animate the scene without implying facial/body performance. Native hero sizes are941×1672 or1024×1536, and the support atlas is recorded above; none is native4K. Portrait crops inherit source resolution. Non-hero atlas cells have lower facial detail than the six hero sources. Physical garment, hair and crown motion would need authored rigged meshes or approved moving footage.
