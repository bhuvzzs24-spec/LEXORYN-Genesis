# LEXORYN — approved visual reference catalog and asset plan

All 14 source images were visually inspected individually before implementation. Source folder: `/Users/bhuvzzs/Downloads/references:` (the colon is part of the real folder name). Original files remain untouched. SHA-256 hashes, exact dimensions and derivative sizes are recorded in `asset-manifest.json`.

## Locked direction

Cinematic realism, monumental graphite/bronze structures, amber practical lighting, restrained cyan instruments, heavy atmospheric depth, weathered mechanical detail and believable material response. Use warm ceremonial architecture and cool industrial spaces as distinct districts of one world. Do not import cartoon portrait styling. No stock assets or new generated scene plates are needed for Phase 1.

## Phase 1 scene map

1. **Orbit / LEXORYN:** original WebGL sphere mesh with procedural 3D mineral terrain, cobalt basins, golden fault emissions, violet cloud layer, lit atmosphere, stars and orbiting structures. No reference image used as the exterior. Borrow material and light language from the atlas, rupture and command references.
2. **Atmospheric entry:** scroll brings the real planet toward camera; the cloud-city plate appears through the transition at its natural proportions. This is a cinematic transition, not a claim of a fully simulated volumetric cloud flight.
3. **Velair Prime:** actual `21.09.45` image, uncropped in its image frame; amber/charcoal visual identity.
4. **Capital arrival dossier:** actual `21.11.01` hangar and `21.11.26` sanctum plates, each at its original aspect ratio, with an accessible full-image viewer.

## Delivery rules

All derivatives are WebP quality 94 at original pixel dimensions, plus 640px maximum-edge responsive copies at quality 90. There is no upscaling, stretching, source mutation or destructive recoloring. Native screenshot borders remain intact. Use `<picture>`/`srcset` where useful; `width:100%;height:auto` preserves ratios. Original screenshots cap effective detail at about 1100px; later higher-resolution masters would improve large-display sharpness.

Future scene generation, if needed, must follow the material palette and environmental physics above, avoid embedded text, keep character identities and region-specific costumes coherent, and provide clean separate plates. Needed later: a clean cloud-flight plate, original Cryonth/Pyravar environments, remaining consistent character portraits and crisis outcome scenes. Do not enlarge the tiny storm thumbnail or use the atlas/collage as scene artwork.
